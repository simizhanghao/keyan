\section{Diagnostic source-free adaptation baselines}
\label{app:tta_diagnostic}

Source-free test-time adaptation is not the focus of this paper, but we report controlled diagnostic runs to document failure under severe cross-receiver collapse.
Entropy-min TTA and pseudo-prototype adaptation were evaluated on unlabeled calibration Block~A in a controlled RX1$\rightarrow$RX2 diagnostic run (one checkpoint seed, one split repeat).

\begin{table}[!t]
\caption{Diagnostic source-free adaptation baselines (RX1$\rightarrow$RX2, file-level accuracy, \%). Controlled diagnostic run only; not aggregated over seeds or directions.}
\label{tab:tta_diagnostic}
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
Method & RX1$\rightarrow$RX2 \\
\midrule
Source classifier (matched run) & 16.7 \\
Entropy-min TTA & 20.8 \\
Pseudo-proto TTA & 8.3 \\
RCPA-T ($K{=}5$, matched run) & 45.8 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

A pseudo-prototype threshold sweep with confidence thresholds $\{0.5,0.7,0.8,0.9,0.95\}$ selects between 1 and 231 pseudo-labeled windows and updates at most 10 of 24 classes.
The best threshold ($0.5$) achieves 16.7\% file-level accuracy, still far below RCPA-T $K{=}5$ (45.8\% on the matched diagnostic run, 58.3\% pooled in the main evaluation).

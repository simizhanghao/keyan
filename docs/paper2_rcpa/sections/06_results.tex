\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Source-only cross-receiver baselines}
Table~\ref{tab:baseline} summarizes source-only performance.
Both directions remain near chance for 24-way file-level classification ($\approx 20\%$).
Unsupervised TTA on unlabeled calibration Block A does not close the gap: entropy-min TTA reaches only $20.8\%$ and pseudo-proto TTA $8.3\%$ on RX1$\rightarrow$RX2 (quick run).

\subsection{RCPA-T main results}
Table~\ref{tab:rcpa} reports the RCPA-T shot curve.
Pooled across directions, RCPA-T improves from $33.1\%$ at $K=1$ to $75.0\%$ at $K=20$, with $+38.2$ percentage points over the source classifier at $K=5$.
Figure~\ref{fig:rcpa_shotcurve} visualizes the shot curve.

\subsection{Ablations and auxiliary analyses}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} shows that source-only prototypes remain weak, target prototypes (RCPA-T) are strongest, and fixed source-target blending (RCPA-B) underperforms RCPA-T.
OOB representation equalization reduces OOB receiver probe from $75.1\%$ to $35.6\%$ but does not improve file-level accuracy beyond RCPA-T, supporting its role as mechanism validation rather than a primary method.

\subsection{Cross-receiver and receiver-agnostic RFFI}
Receiver-agnostic training with adversarial learning has been proposed to reduce receiver sensitivity, often requiring multi-receiver training or collaborative inference~\cite{shen2023receiveragnostic}.
Source-free cross-receiver adaptation methods instead adapt a pre-trained model using target-receiver unlabeled data and pseudo labels.
Our work differs by providing an OOB-entanglement diagnosis on a frozen hybrid backbone and showing that minimal \emph{labeled} target calibration windows can be more reliable than unsupervised adaptation under severe collapse.

\subsection{Few-shot and prototype classification}
Prototypical classification is a classical few-shot approach~\cite{snell2017prototypical}.
We do not claim a new prototype algorithm; rather, we introduce a diagnosis-driven cross-receiver calibration protocol with block-disjoint window splits tailored to LoRa single-file-per-device captures.

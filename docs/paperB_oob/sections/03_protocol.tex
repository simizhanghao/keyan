\section{Auditable Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}
\subsection{Dataset and Split}
We use the public 20-SDR LoRa dataset introduced for cross-receiver RFFI studies, containing ten transmitter devices and twenty receiver domains. Fourteen receiver files form the development pool, with 8,000 training packets per receiver. Six official receiver files are held out as blind test domains: b200\_2, b200\_mini\_2, b210\_2, n210\_2, n210\_3 and pluto\_2. Each blind receiver contains 2,000 test packets, with 200 packets from each of ten devices. The blind archive hash and file inventory were recorded before signal values were opened. The source/blind boundary was not used for training, validation, checkpoint selection, intervention design, or hyperparameter selection.

\subsection{OOB Definition and Signal Audit}
Signals are normalized per packet by their root-mean-square complex amplitude. With sampling rate $F_s=1$ MHz and nominal LoRa bandwidth $B=125$ kHz, the in-band set is $|f|\leq B/2$ and OOB is its complement. We summarize each packet by the relative OOB magnitude $20\log_{10}((\mathrm{RMS}_{\mathrm{OOB}}+\epsilon)/(\mathrm{RMS}_{\mathrm{IB}}+\epsilon))$. The signal audit groups observations by device, receiver type, receiver instance and day. Because the source HDF5 files do not expose capture identifiers, this audit is descriptive rather than a capture-level inferential test.

\subsection{Frozen Backbones and Controls}
The primary probe models are denoted descriptively as LateFusion-OOB, an input- and capacity-matched late-fusion CNN, and CrossAttn-OOB, an HSTU-inspired RF token encoder adapted from Zhai \emph{et al.}~\cite{zhai2024actions} with cross-attentive OOB fusion. HSTU is used here as a probe backbone rather than as an architectural contribution. CrossAttn-TrueIB uses the same family with a strict in-band spectral path and is a pathway control, not a claim that all OOB effects are removed from every representation. Both OOB-aware models use AdamW, learning rate $10^{-3}$, weight decay $5\times10^{-4}$, and batch size 64. Development audits use six held-out folds and two seeds. Final confirmatory training uses all fourteen development receivers, five seeds, no validation or early stopping, and fixed epochs: 5 (LateFusion-OOB), 4 (CrossAttn-OOB), 5 (CrossAttn-TrueIB), 74 (Shen-CIS), and 71 (Shen-RA). The 25 canonical checkpoints were then sealed. Shen-CIS uses CIS inputs of shape $52\times126$, about 13.51M parameters, SGD with learning rate $10^{-3}$ and momentum 0.9; Shen-RA adds a gradient-reversal layer with a 1:1 transmitter/receiver loss. These are faithful PyTorch adaptations, not exact reproductions of external TensorFlow code.

\subsection{Interventions}
For an OOB-aware model, the clean main I/Q branch and device label remain unchanged. Scale interventions multiply OOB spectral bins by $\{0.5,0.70710678,1,1.41421356,2\}$, with scale 1 as clean. We define worst-scale drop as clean accuracy minus the minimum accuracy over the prespecified non-unit grid; the grid was fixed before blind opening. Shuffle replaces OOB bins with a different-device donor from the same receiver file. Donor labels are used only after blind opening to construct this inference-time mismatch and never for training, checkpoint selection, or model selection. Neutral replacement uses a development-derived, equal-receiver-weighted OOB magnitude vector and zero phase. Left/right interventions multiply only the negative- or positive-frequency OOB half by 0.5. These interventions probe learned OOB dependencies; they are not interpreted as a complete causal decomposition of receiver variation.

\subsection{Freeze and Statistical Analysis}
Twenty-five canonical checkpoints were strictly loaded and hashed before the protocol-frozen confirmatory blind evaluation. The six blind receiver files were then opened once. The evaluation covers five models, five seeds and six receivers under clean conditions, and the two OOB-aware models under eight frozen intervention conditions, for 630 runs. Accuracy and Macro-F1 are computed per run. For inference, seed means are formed within receiver before aggregation; the receiver/domain is the primary statistical unit. The prespecified confirmatory criteria require positive disruption and worst-scale drops on at least four of six receivers with receiver medians of at least 5 percentage points, plus positive left-minus-right effects on at least four receivers with a positive receiver median. Both backbones satisfied all criteria.

\subsection{Probe Protocol}
For device and receiver probes, the learned embedding is frozen and only a lightweight classifier is fitted. Device-disjoint and receiver-disjoint splits prevent leakage across the corresponding grouping variable. Each receiver--device cell contributes 256 packets; receiver chance level is $1/13=7.7\%$. Probe training uses fixed seeds and the same source/development boundary as the main study. These probes are descriptive tests of information content, not causal tests.

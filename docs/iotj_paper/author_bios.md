# Author Biographies (Not included in initial submission PDF)

## Ziyang Wang

Ziyang Wang received the Ph.D. degree in electromagnetic field and microwave technology in 2018. He was previously a postdoctoral fellow at Tsinghua University. He is currently an Associate Professor with the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beihang University, Beijing, China. His research interests include intelligent metasurfaces, broadband reconfigurable element design, phase-controlled electromagnetic surfaces, spatial beamforming design, global optimization algorithms, and 5G MIMO array antenna decoupling.

## Chengcheng Han

Chengcheng Han is currently pursuing the M.S. degree in engineering with the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beihang University, Beijing, China. His research interests include high-temperature electromagnetic environments and electromagnetic compatibility. He is the corresponding author of this paper and can be contacted at HAN106124@163.com.

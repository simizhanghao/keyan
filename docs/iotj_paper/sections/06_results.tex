\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Cross-Day Main Results}
Table~\ref{tab:cross_day_main} and Fig.~\ref{fig:cross_day_seeds} summarize the primary cross-day comparison.
The proposed hybrid achieves \textbf{75.0$\pm$5.3\%} file-level accuracy versus \textbf{54.2$\pm$14.2\%} for CNN-IQ, with mean paired gain \textbf{+20.8} percentage points and pooled bootstrap 95\% CI \textbf{[+9.2, +32.5]} pp.
Across five seeds the hybrid wins four times, ties once, and never loses.
CNN-IQ exhibits higher seed variance, indicating less stable cross-day behavior.

\subsection{Fusion and Chirp Ablations}
Table~\ref{tab:fusion_chirp} and Fig.~\ref{fig:fusion_ablation} confirm \textbf{Case A}: cross-attention dominates fusion choice.
Cross-attention with or without chirp embeddings both reach about 75\% file accuracy, whereas concat fusion remains near chance for multiple seeds (9.7--18.3\%).
Adding chirp to concat fusion does not rescue performance.
We therefore attribute the main gain to \emph{cross-attentive OOB fusion}, not chirp embedding alone.

\subsection{Deployment-Shift Results}
Table~\ref{tab:deployment_shift} reports LOCO deployment-shift results.
Hybrid models outperform CNN-IQ on held-out distance bins at 10/15/20\,m and on indoor room/office locations.
Outdoor location shift remains difficult and CNN-IQ can exceed the hybrid model.
Configuration shifts are challenging for both models.
These results support \emph{partial} deployment robustness rather than universal invariance.

\subsection{Edge Deployment Profile}
Table~\ref{tab:edge_deployment} shows the hybrid model is heavier (1.16\,M vs 47.7\,K parameters) yet still achieves about 2.45\,ms single-window latency on the benchmark GPU setup, suggesting feasibility for edge gateways with modest compute headroom.

\subsection{Cross-Receiver Stress Test}
Strict source-only cross-receiver transfer remains near the 4.17\% chance level for 24-way file classification (Table~\ref{tab:cross_receiver}, Fig.~\ref{fig:cross_receiver}).
Hybrid models improve the hard RX1$\rightarrow$RX2 direction (18.1\% vs 4.2\% mean file accuracy) but do not uniformly dominate CNN-IQ in the reverse direction.
Same-receiver upper bounds from prior diagnostics reach much higher accuracy, indicating receiver calibration mismatch as the dominant bottleneck rather than lack of separability.
